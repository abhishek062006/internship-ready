DATASET / CSV

Train & evaluate

At least 50 unique profiles, 15 per class. Maximum 10,000 rows and 2 MB. Blank numeric cells are imputed from training data. In coding_languages, separate names with | (for example Python|Java). Leave it blank for no languages. Labels must be observed outcomes: 0 = not selected, 1 = selected.

EVALUATION PROTOCOL

A test set kept separate

  1. Validate & deduplicate

    Reject invalid ranges and conflicting labels. Remove identical profiles.

  2. Split 80% / 20%

    Stratified split with seed 42. Fit imputation and scaling inside training folds.

  3. Compare on training data

    Three-fold cross-validation selects the highest mean F1 from logistic regression, decision tree, random forest, and SVM.

  4. Evaluate the chosen model

    Report accuracy, precision, recall, F1, and confusion matrix on the untouched 20% test set.

PROJECT / TEAM 06

Student Internship
Selection Prediction

A working implementation of the preparation and classification workflow proposed in the academic deck.

From proposal to working application

The deck describes uncertainty around internship readiness and proposes using academic performance, technical skills, projects, certifications, aptitude, communication, internship experience, and interview performance to classify students as “Likely to be Selected” or “Not Selected.”

Implemented

Seven numeric inputs and coding-language selections, validated CSV ingestion, duplicate handling, training-only median imputation and scaling, four classifier comparison, held-out evaluation, prediction, permutation importance, local what-if effects, and benchmark-based strengths and improvement recommendations.

Planned / outside this build

XGBoost, automated feature selection, SMOTE, company-specific models, accounts, longitudinal tracking, external integrations, and production deployment remain future work. Each supported coding language is a separate model input. Class weighting handles imbalance without generating artificial records.

How to interpret the guidance

The deck does not define feature scales or readiness cutoffs. This implementation uses CGPA out of 10, aptitude, communication and interview scores out of 100, counts of projects and certifications, and completed internship months. Readiness benchmarks are explicit product assumptions, not validated selection criteria. The readiness index averages progress toward seven numeric benchmarks and caps each factor at 100%. Coding languages are recorded separately without treating language count as proficiency.

Model probabilities are uncalibrated estimates, not a guarantee of selection. Feature importance and what-if effects describe model behavior, not causation. Real hiring decisions require human judgment. Historical labels can encode bias and may not generalize across companies, cohorts, or regions.
